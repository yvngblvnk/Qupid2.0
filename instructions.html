How it works

Pen, paper,
and presence.

Qupid gives you the questions. You bring the sticky notes, a pen, and each other. The rest happens offline — in real life, between real people.

🗒️

Before you start, grab sticky notes and a pen or two. The phone shows the questions — you answer them by hand, together.

The flow

1

A question appears on screen

Both of you read it together. The card's colour shows which level it is — casual, personal, or very personal.

2

Put the phone down and write

Place the phone face-down. Each person writes their honest answer on their own sticky note — privately, without seeing what the other is writing.

You

When we drive with no destination and just talk…

✍️

Them

Quiet mornings before the day pulls us apart…

3

Swap notes — read, don't react yet

Hand your sticky note to your partner and take theirs. Read it slowly. Sit with it before you speak.

4

Pick up the phone — start the timer

Tap "Start discussion" and talk about what you both wrote. When the timer ends, a gentle chime plays and the next question is ready.

Discussion time

5

Next question appears automatically

Once the timer is up, the next question loads. Hold onto your notes — by the end you'll have a paper record of this whole conversation.

Question levels

Casual

Light, fun prompts. Everyday preferences and playful opinions. A comfortable warm-up.

Personal

Reflective questions about values, memories, and how you see the world.

Very personal

Deep, vulnerable prompts about fears, hopes, and what truly matters. Go gently.

Levels rotate in a random order — you won't always know the depth of what's coming next.

Ground rules

🤫

Write in secret. Phone goes face-down while you write. No peeking at each other's note until the swap.

⏩

Skipping is allowed. Either person can pass on any question, no explanation needed.

🕊️

No wrong answers. No scores, no judgement — just two people being honest.

📌

Keep the notes. They're a paper record of this moment. You may want to read them again someday.

Make sure you both have sticky notes and a pen before you begin.
